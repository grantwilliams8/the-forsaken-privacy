The Forsaken – Support

Help for the iPhone and iPad game · Developer: Grant Williams

Something not working, or stuck in the manor? Start with the answers below. If they don't help, email hello@saventa.com.au and say which iPhone or iPad and which iOS version you have.

I bought the full game or hint tokens but they're missing

Open Menu → Store (or Menu → Settings) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. Restoring never charges you again.

How do hints work?

Tap HINT at the top of the game screen. The first levels of each hint are free. Stronger hints use hint tokens, which are an optional in-app purchase. Hints are never required to finish the game.

I'm stuck and can't find the way forward

Check your Journal for clues, people and objectives, and your Map to see rooms you haven't explored. Try examining objects more than once and using or combining items from your Inventory. The first mystery ends at the cellar stairs, where the rest of the story can be unlocked.

Where is my saved game?

The game saves automatically on your device and needs no account. Choose Continue on the title screen to pick up where you left off. Saves live on the device, so they are removed if you delete the app, unless your device backup restores them. To start over, use Menu → Settings → Reset Save. Your purchases and the endings you've found are kept.

Text is too small, or the screen is hard to read

Open Menu → Settings to change the text size and turn on high-contrast mode. Sound and haptics can be changed there too.

Report a problem

Email hello@saventa.com.au with a short description of what happened, what you were doing in the game, and your device and iOS version. A screenshot helps.
